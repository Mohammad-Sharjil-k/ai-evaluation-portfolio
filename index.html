Benchmark Case Study: Multi-Constraint Prompt Testing

1. Target Test Prompt

"Summarize the working mechanism of photosynthesis for a middle school student.
Constraints: 1) Exactly 3 bullet points. 2) Total length must be strictly between 50 and 70 words. 3) Do NOT use the words 'plant', 'green', or 'sunlight'. 4) Each bullet must begin with a active verb."

4. Final Verdict & Justification

Winner: Model A

Justification Rationale: Model A is strictly superior due to 100% adherence to all positive and negative constraints. Model B failed Constraint #2 by falling below the required length threshold (generating 44 words against the mandatory 50–70 range). Both models correctly navigated negative constraints without sacrificing scientific accuracy, but Model A demonstrated superior precise length control while maintaining an accessible tone for middle school readers.
